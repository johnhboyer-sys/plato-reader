## Legend

Line format: `Author, Title — Publisher (Series), year first (cur. year of the in-print printing) · ISBN-13 · format · Greek: ... · stock · link [verification]`

Greek field: `only` (Greek text, no translation) · `facing EN` / `facing FR` (Greek with facing translation) · `commentary` (Greek text with notes, no translation) · `no` (translation only).

Verification tags:
- `[V]` publisher page fetched; ISBN and details read from it.
- `[V-redir]` publisher ISBN URL resolves to the right product (Cambridge: `cambridge.org/<ISBN>` redirects to the product slug; the page itself sits behind a bot check, so details come from Open Library and search listings).
- `[V-OL]` ISBN and title confirmed in an Open Library record (link points there); publisher page not fetched. WorldCat refused automated access (HTTP 429), so Open Library stands in for the fallback record.
- `[V-search]` publisher URL returned by a web search with the ISBN in the URL, ISBN also confirmed in Open Library; page not fetched.
- `[UNVERIFIED]` could not confirm; kept rather than dropped.

Notes on dates: "first" years not shown on a publisher page come from standard bibliography and are marked `~`. Stock status: HUP product pages all carry an "out of stock" flag when fetched (including a 2025 title), so for the Loeb volumes "in print" means "listed in the HUP catalogue as a current hardcover"; confirm stock before linking as "buy".

Hosted on the site: **[HOSTED]** = the translation shown on plato.johnhboyer.com (Loeb translators Fowler, Lamb, Shorey, Bury; Jowett compare column on 11 dialogues: Cratylus, Crito, Euthyphro, Gorgias, Ion, Laches, Meno, Phaedrus, Philebus, Sophist, Theaetetus, per `sources/jowett-*`).
